PROBLEMS FACED

PROPOSED SOLUTION

Deliveries often clash with work hours causing repeat
attempts or Post Office visits due to recipient absence.
Re-attempts waste Postman time and delivery capacity.
Failed deliveries cause dissatisfaction and return to sender.
Potential untapped for newer deliveries to be carried out,
which could make deliveries faster for everyone else.

Slot-booking service within India Post’s Advanced
Postal Technology (APT) system.
Sender chooses preferred delivery window.
Recipient (&sender) can edit it before delivery day.
Multi-channel access via Web, WhatsApp & SMS.

HOW IT SOLVES THE PROBLEM

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

DIGIPIN (4m x 4m digital grid address) provides
precise location for every address to AI engine.
Traffic, weather, construction and festivals are
considered to adjust the routing.
Each slot is checked against the postman’s route.
Reduces repeated delivery attempts and
re-delivery workload.

Route-aware slots prevent postman overload.
We show only route-feasible slots.
Customers see the trade-off and choose the
window with higher success.
Counterfactual slots compare possible delivery
windows. (chosen slot v/s best alternative)
